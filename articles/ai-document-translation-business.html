Why Business Translation Costs Are Unsustainable

Globalization means more translation needs than ever, but traditional translation pricing makes scaling multilingual operations prohibitively expensive:

  • Cost per word adds up fast: Professional human translation costs $0.10-$0.30/word. A 50-page business document (~15,000 words) costs $1,500-$4,500. Multiply by 5 target languages and you're at $7,500-$22,500 for one document
  • Speed bottleneck: Human translators produce 2,000-3,000 words/day. That 50-page document takes 5-8 business days per language. Urgent translations command 50-100% rush premiums
  • Inconsistency across translators: Different translators use different terminology for the same technical terms, creating confusion in multilingual documentation sets
  • Revision cycles: Translation → review → revision → re-review cycles add 30-50% to timelines and costs
  • Scaling impossibility: Companies launching in 10+ markets need to translate websites, apps, documentation, legal agreements, and marketing — the volume overwhelms any human translation budget

True cost of traditional translation (company expanding to 5 markets):

  • Website translation (50 pages × 5 languages): $37,500-$112,500
  • Product documentation (200 pages × 5 languages): $150,000-$450,000
  • Legal contracts (50 pages × 5 languages): $37,500-$112,500
  • Marketing materials (30 pages × 5 languages): $22,500-$67,500
  • Ongoing updates and maintenance: $50,000-$150,000/year
  • Total first-year: $297,500-$892,500 — AI reduces this by 60-85%

When AI Translation Needs Human Backup

AI translation is transformative, but there are situations where human involvement remains essential:

Always Needs Human Review

  • Legal contracts: Mistranslation of a liability clause or warranty term can create legal exposure. Always use certified human translation or AI + professional post-editing
  • Medical/pharmaceutical: Patient-facing materials, clinical trial documents, and regulatory submissions require certified accuracy
  • Marketing copy with cultural nuance: Taglines, brand voice, humor, and culturally-specific references require human transcreation, not translation
  • Regulatory filings: SEC, EU regulatory, and government submission documents often require certified translations by accredited translators

AI-Only Is Usually Fine

  • Internal communications: Emails, meeting notes, internal reports — understanding is the goal, not perfection
  • Technical documentation: User manuals, API documentation, help center articles — with glossary-controlled terminology
  • E-commerce product descriptions: With post-editing for tone and cultural fit
  • Customer support responses: Real-time translation of support tickets and chat with human agent review
  • Market research: Understanding foreign-language competitor content, news, and customer reviews

The Hybrid Workflow: AI + Human Post-Editing (MTPE)

The most cost-effective approach for professional-quality translation is Machine Translation Post-Editing (MTPE):

  1. AI translates the full document (seconds to minutes)
  2. Professional translator reviews and edits the AI output (50-70% faster than translating from scratch)
  3. Quality assurance check by a second reviewer for critical documents

MTPE cost comparison:

  • Full human translation: $0.10-$0.30/word
  • MTPE (light editing): $0.04-$0.08/word — 60-70% cheaper
  • MTPE (full editing): $0.06-$0.12/word — 40-60% cheaper
  • AI-only (no human review): $0.001-$0.005/word — 95-99% cheaper

Smartcat offers the best integrated MTPE workflow — AI translation feeds directly into a human post-editing interface with quality metrics, translator assignments, and review tracking in one platform.

Excellent Quality (90-97% accuracy, minimal post-editing needed)

  • Any pair between major European languages

Good Quality (85-93% accuracy, moderate post-editing)

  • English ↔ Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional)
  • English ↔ Russian, Turkish, Arabic
  • English ↔ Hindi, Thai, Vietnamese

Developing Quality (75-87% accuracy, significant review needed)

  • Less-common language pairs (e.g., Japanese ↔ Arabic, Korean ↔ Portuguese)
  • Languages with fewer digital training resources (Swahili, Urdu, Bengali)
  • Highly inflected or agglutinative languages (Finnish, Hungarian, Turkish from non-English)

Security and Confidentiality Considerations

Business documents often contain sensitive information. Here's how AI translation platforms handle confidentiality:

  • DeepL Pro: Documents are encrypted in transit and at rest. Pro subscribers' content is not used for model training. Enterprise plans offer additional data processing agreements and GDPR compliance documentation
  • Google Cloud Translation: SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified. Data processing agreements available. Content processed via the paid API is not used to train models (unlike the free version)
  • ModernMT: Offers on-premise deployment for maximum confidentiality — translation happens entirely within your infrastructure with zero external data transmission
  • Smartcat: Enterprise plans include dedicated servers, SSO, and custom data retention policies

Critical rule: Never use free translation tools (Google Translate web, DeepL free) for confidential business documents. Free tiers may use your content for model training. Always use paid API or enterprise plans for sensitive content, and verify the vendor's data processing terms before uploading anything confidential.
